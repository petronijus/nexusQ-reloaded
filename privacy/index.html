Nexus Q Reloaded

Privacy Policy · effective 2026-10-11

Nexus Q Reloaded is the companion app for Google Nexus Q devices running the Nexus Q Reloaded firmware. It is an independent open-source project and has no server of its own. This policy covers the app on Android and iOS.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no account of its own. It sends no data to the developer or to any third party, other than to the services below that you choose to use.

Where the app connects

  • Your Nexus Q, on your local network. The app finds the Q on your home network and sends it your commands: playback, volume, equalizer, settings and updates. When you set up a Q, the WiFi network name and password you enter are sent to that Q so that it can join your network; the app does not keep the password.
  • Spotify, only if you link a Spotify account. The app signs in with Spotify and uses Spotify's Web API to show and control what is playing. The sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage and are sent to nobody but Spotify. Spotify's privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Your own MQTT broker, only if you set one up. The health view reads your Q's telemetry from a broker you run, such as the one Home Assistant uses. Its address and login are kept in your phone's secure storage.
  • GitHub, only in the version downloaded from GitHub. That version checks GitHub for a newer app and downloads it from there. Copies installed from Google Play or F-Droid do not.

What stays on your phone

The addresses of your Qs, your settings and the logins above. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth — to set up a Q and to pair devices with it. Bluetooth scans are not used to determine your location.
  • Location — on Android 11 and older only, which require it for Bluetooth scanning.
  • NFC — only during setup, for tap-to-set-up.
  • Notifications — to tell you when your Q has a new release.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.
